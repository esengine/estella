**The Tile Palette and Flipbook Editor use the same dropdowns as the viewport toolbar.** Long tileset names wrap, and the actions to open or remove a tileset stay visible on touch screens. Both menus mark the current choice with a check and support arrow keys, Escape and scrolling in short windows. The Flipbook playback bar keeps its button labels together when docked in a narrow panel.
