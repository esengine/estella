**The hierarchy is a relationship the engine stores itself, and world transforms compose 25% faster in a scene of trees.** Each entity's parent, its first and last child, its siblings and its depth are kept in flat arrays indexed by entity, in the manner of Flecs' (ChildOf, parent) pairs: setting a parent, detaching, and reordering children cost the same however many children there are, and allocate nothing. World transforms compose by walking every tree as one array in which each parent comes before its children, with no stack and no per-entity lookups of a child list: 100,000 entities in 1,000 trees compose in 2.6 ms where they took 3.4 ms. Removing a child no longer reorders its siblings. The `Parent` and `Children` components are still there for scripts and scene files, kept in step with the relation; the relation is what the engine reads, so a `Parent` written into a component without going through the hierarchy's own edit links nothing.
