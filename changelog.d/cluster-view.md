**The viewport can show which level of detail each part of a Cluster LOD mesh is drawn at.** Pick **Clusters** in the viewport's shading menu and every cluster is drawn in a colour of its own over clay: small patches where the camera is close, large ones far away. A mesh without clusters is plain clay.
