**The Details panel is laid out as Unreal's.** A component's title now follows its arrow directly, with the box that turns it
off at the right end of the header, so titles no longer start at different places depending on whether a component can be
switched off. X, Y and Z fields are told apart by a colour bar down the left of an unoutlined field, without a letter (width,
height and box sides keep theirs). Advanced fields fold behind a thin bar with a chevron along the bottom of the component and
open above it.
