**The sound events page now lists every `.esaudioevent` field and the playback API.** Each field has its type, default and limits (`bus`, `priority`, `maxInstances`, `cooldown`, `spatial` were not described before). A reference covers `SoundEvents.load` and its options, the emitter, the `SoundEventHandle` statuses and every rejection reason, the `SoundEventStats` counters and `getVariantShares`. In English and Chinese.
