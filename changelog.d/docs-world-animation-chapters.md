**The World and Animation documentation is rewritten as reference pages, in English and Chinese.** Tilemaps and
Particles are now groups of pages, Animation gains Sprite Animation, Tweens, Animator, Animation Events and Layers, IK &
Root Motion pages, and Timeline is a group of Sequencer, Tracks and Playback pages; old addresses redirect to the new
pages. Component fields are listed with their types and defaults, and statements were checked against the code and
corrected where they had drifted — among them tween `loopCount`, Spine `setEntityProps` and shared mix durations, the
Preview FX location, GPU trail memory, force fields on GPU emitters, the collision layer's grid and physics material,
and prefab override fields.
