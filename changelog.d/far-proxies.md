**A streamed world draws its distant places: each cell can ship a far proxy that stands in for it while it is not loaded.** Tick **Far proxies** on `StreamedWorld` and a build bakes every cell a stand-in from its static, opaque meshes, prefab instances included: merged into one draw per material (surfaces that differ only in colour share it, the colour carried by the vertices) and simplified as far as `proxyError` world units allow, `proxyRatio` being the fewest triangles to aim for. While a source is within `farDistance` of a cell that is not resident, the cell's proxy is drawn in its place. The hand-over leaves no gap in either direction: walking towards a cell, its proxy stays until the cell is in the world, and walking away, the cell stays until its proxy is. Play makes the same proxies with the build's own simplifier, each when the streamer first asks for it, so it starts as soon as before. In the new `far-proxy-town` example (144 cells, 3.1 million triangles of static meshes) a frame along its flight draws 122 thousand triangles in 354 draws where the town fully loaded draws 866 thousand in 620, with 0.3% of the pixels noticeably different. Off by default; the cooked manifest names each proxy (`WorldCell.proxy`, `WorldManifest.farDistance`), and `WorldStreamerStatus.proxyCells` lists the cells drawn by one.
