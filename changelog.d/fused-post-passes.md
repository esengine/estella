**Colour grade, sharpen, film grain and vignette side by side are drawn as one pass.** Effects that work on a pixel at a time and stand next to each other in a volume are now one full-screen pass instead of one each, which is a whole picture read and written less per effect on a phone's GPU. Each keeps its own params and its volume's weight, sharpen reads each of its four neighbours through the effects before it as it read their output, and an effect that a quality level, a player's settings or the volume turns off is left out of the pass. On the space frigate's low level at 2400 x 1080 the four passes took 0.235 ms and the one takes 0.139 ms (0.388 to 0.248 ms at high), and the frame matches the separate passes to within 2/255. `PostProcessAPI.fusePasses = false` draws them separately again, for a profile of what each costs alone.
