**Shaders are written once, in GLSL.** A shader with no WGSL of its own is given it as it compiles wherever the engine draws through WebGPU — a web game set to WebGPU, the native host and the editor — by a transpiler that keeps its `#ifdef`s. Twelve built-in shaders (Model and the Ground Blend and Voxel Ground made from it, Toon, Matcap, Skin, Hair, Eye, Particle, Lit Particle, Distortion and Foliage's surface) now ship as GLSL alone, 34 KB less shader text, and draw on WebGPU as they did. A package that draws only through WebGL carries none of the transpiler.
