**A camera impulse source shows its reach in the viewport.** Selected, a CameraImpulseSource draws a sphere where its jolt is felt at full strength and a dimmer one where it has faded to nothing, as Cinemachine draws an Impulse Source, so a blast is placed by what it will reach. Before, its radius and dissipation were numbers with nothing to see.
