**Editable inspector dropdowns support keyboard selection and input methods.** Use the arrow keys to highlight a suggestion, Enter to choose it, or Tab to finish typing and move to another property. Editing a localization key now shows its stored spelling, keeps the translation in the option list, and lets an input method finish composing before saving.
