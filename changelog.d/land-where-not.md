**A land's `where` can keep places out.** `not` takes a `where`, or a list of them, and none of a map, layer, tint or step
lies where one holds, its edge as soft as that `where`'s own: a wood that leaves room for the towns in it, or a clearing,
without cutting the outline round each one.
