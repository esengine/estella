**A web game can ship its models as recipes and bake them on the player's graphics card.** A model source whose `.meta` says `"bake": "load"` is left out of a web export as a mesh and maps and travels as its text instead: this game's 50 MB of baked maps became a 109 KB file. Where the game loads, workers expand each recipe and fragment shaders bake its normal, colour, surface, emissive and occlusion maps, at a size the device sets (4096 on a desktop, 2048 on a strong phone, 1024 and 512 below that; `?bake=2048` asks for another), and the result is kept in the browser so the next launch reads it back. The maps come out the same as the build-time bake's. A game shows the wait itself: `loadWork().run(onProgress)` reports it, and a game that never asks has the models baked when its first scene needs them.
