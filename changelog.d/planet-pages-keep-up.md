**A planet's ground fills in to its finest under a moving eye, where it stayed a few flat facets.** Every frame the workers already had as many pages as they are given at once, the planet asked for one more and threw its answer away, so the workers spent their time on pages nobody kept: flying low over a planet at speed, the ground was still drawn from its two coarsest levels after twenty seconds, mountains were flat-sided, and what a world placed on the true ground (trees, rocks) hung in the air over it. Pages are now asked for only when there is room, are spread over the worker threads instead of queuing on the one their face named, and go ahead of the laying out of a world's plants: a pool gives its first half of workers to the ground and the rest to everything else, and holds what a worker has not started so that urgent work goes first. A machine of more than eight cores starts half its cores as workers, eight at most, where it started four. Measured over a planet of radius 58,000 at full detail, flying at 210 units a second: the ground whole in 10 to 15 s and whole from then on.
