**A world laid out on a planet stays on its ground when the planet is moved, and its far plants lean with the planet.** A game that keeps the eye at the origin and moves the planet past it had each chunk of plants set down off the ground by how far the planet had moved between the chunk being worked out and being published. The far cards of a plant (two crossed cards, or a tent) were built along the world's up, so away from the top of a planet they lay over; they now lean as the plant's mesh does.
