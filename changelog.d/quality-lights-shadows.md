**Lights and shadows have per-level settings.** Under **Lights & Shading**: **Per-pixel lights** (8 / 12 / 16) and **Tiled lights** (32 / 64 / 96, with Full lighting). Under **Shadows**: **Shadow maps** a frame draws (8 / 12 / 16, a point light takes six), and, for soft 3D shadows with Full lighting, **search samples** (8 / 8 / 16), **filter samples** (8 / 16 / 32) and **max radius** (12 / 16 / 24 texels). Under **Global Illumination & Occlusion**: **Contact shadow steps** (8 / 12 / 20). High is what the engine drew before. A Light also gains **Shadow Bias** (1.5 texels), **Shadow Slope Floor** (0.15) and, on a Directional light, **Cascade Split** (0.75), at the values that were fixed in the engine.
