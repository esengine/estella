**Rain splashes are particles in the world, its rings are a raindrop's, and how all of it looks is the Rain effect's to set.** A
splash was drawn on the screen; it is now a bright point and droplets thrown up where a drop lands, falling back under the project's
gravity, on ground, roofs and water alike. The rings on water and on puddles are small, fast and many, one clear crest with a fainter
one behind, and rings that meet add and cancel; water and puddles ring where the splashes land, from one shared landing pattern. The
falling drops stay a dense system of their own. Nothing of the look is fixed: the Rain effect sets the streaks' width and brightness,
the flakes' size, the splashes' droplets, size, speed, life and colour, and the rings' speed, life, width and strength, and takes a
texture for streaks, flakes and splashes, so a toon rain is the author's values, not new code.
