**A replication server no longer takes whatever a client sends.** Its connections had none of the protections the session layer's have: a client could send frames of any size and messages at any rate, hold a connection without ever saying hello, or vanish behind a half-open link that the server never noticed. Every connection now has limits by default — frames over 256 KiB are dropped unread, control messages past 120 a second are refused and a connection that keeps breaking either limit is kicked, a connection that says nothing for 15 s (pings every 5 s) or no hello in 10 s is closed, and a second hello is refused — and `maxConnections` turns newcomers away as "server full, try again". `webSocketTransport` closes a client more than 4 MiB behind on reading instead of buffering for it without end, and the client pings too, so a server that vanished without closing is noticed and redialed. Tune them on `startServer({ limits, heartbeat, helloTimeoutMs, maxConnections })` and `webSocketTransport(socket, { maxBufferedBytes })`.
