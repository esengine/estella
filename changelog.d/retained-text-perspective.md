**Text in a perspective view is no longer laid out again every frame.** A label of automatic render mode under a perspective camera, or in a screen overlay given a perspective, is drawn with SDF glyphs whatever its scale; the engine's check for a stale label knew only the rule by scale, called every such label stale every frame, and each was laid out again each time. A HUD of a hundred and fifty labels allocated 23 MB a frame doing it and ran at a third of its frame rate. The engine now judges by scale only the labels whose pipeline scale chose.
