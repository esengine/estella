**A screen UI that did not change is drawn from what the engine already holds, not built again every frame.** The screen overlay used to collect every control, mask and label, re-transform every glyph, sort, merge and upload its vertices each frame, though a HUD is still most of the time. It now has a pool and draw list of its own and keeps them: while no component of an entity in the screen's UI was written, added or removed, and nothing it was built from moved (the projection, the layers, the materials, a texture's size or nine-slice borders, a font, a label's text), the pass draws what it holds and nothing is collected or uploaded. A moving world, a moving camera or anything outside the screen's UI does not stop it. A particle emitter or a Spine or DragonBones skeleton in the screen's UI rebuilds the pass every frame, as before. A `Text` changed in place on the object `world.get` returned, without `world.set` or `world.insert`, no longer shows the change while the pass is held: `world.get` already documents writing to its result as unobserved, and every example writes a label back. On the UI list example the text work of a frame fell from 0.29 to 0.12 ms with the CPU slowed five times, and the GL calls of a frame from 74 to 61. The change audit (`World.setChangeAudit`) builds every pass and counts one that differs from what would have been held (`render.overlay.stale`), and `render.overlay.kept` and `render.overlay.why` say whether a frame was held and what stopped it.
