**The Sequencer zooms.** Ctrl+wheel over the lanes zooms the time axis about the pointer, the ruler drawing finer ticks as it goes; the lanes scroll sideways, follow the playhead while playing, and Fit brings the whole timeline back. Markers are drawn on the ruler by name, and a click jumps the playhead to one, as UE's marked frames do. A long timeline was a strip of keys too close to tell apart before.
