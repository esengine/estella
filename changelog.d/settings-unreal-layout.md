**Settings looks and works like Unreal's Editor Preferences, and its colour pickers close properly.** Each page has a title, each group sits under a header bar that folds it, and the rows form a property grid with names and values parted by a line. A colour picker opened in Settings or another dialog now closes when you click elsewhere or press Enter, not only with Escape.
