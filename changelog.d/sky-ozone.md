**A low sun's sky lights the scene blue, not olive.** The Sky Atmosphere scattered light through air and haze but had no ozone, which in the real sky absorbs orange and green along the long, low path of a sun near the horizon. Without it a dusk sky came out yellow-green and, with Light the Scene on, lit level ground green and a wall turned from the sun olive. The dome, its clouds' light, the sun's colour and the environment the sky lights the scene with now pass through an ozone layer 25 km up (absorption 0.650, 1.881, 0.085 × 10⁻⁶ per metre, as physically based sky models use), so twilight shade is blue-grey and the dome above a setting sun turns mauve. A noon sky changes by a few percent.
