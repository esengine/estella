**3D snow falls in the world.** The rain effect's snowfall drew flakes as layers on the screen, drifting down the picture whichever
way the camera looked. Snow now falls the way the rain's drops do: flakes in the world round the camera, a metre a second, each
swaying on its own path, carried further by the Wind than rain, soft and round at any angle. They stop on what stands over them, so
none fall under a roof, and anything nearer the camera hides them. As many fall as the snowfall says: a Snow weather fills the air
twice as densely as a light one. The snow lying on the ground is drawn as before.
