**Text is laid out once a frame, only where something changed, and drawn by the engine in the same collect as every other UI element.** A label's glyphs were laid out and handed over from inside each camera's pass and the screen overlay's, which walked every `Text` twice a frame (once per pass, and once more per extra camera) comparing each label's thirty-odd fields. Labels are now laid out by `TextLayoutSystem`, just before the frame is drawn, in one pass over both domains that runs only when a `Text` was written or added, an entity switched off, a font registered or released, the device pixel ratio, a domain's view or an atlas changed, or the engine reports a label whose box, domain or scale moved; a still frame walks no label. The engine draws what it holds in its own collect, after every other plugin, where the script's glyphs used to land. A large label (one drawn from the large-glyph atlas) was reported stale and laid out again on every frame, because the engine was told the atlas's format where it asked what the world scale had chosen; it is laid out once. On the screen text fixture, with the CPU slowed five times, the text work of a frame fell from 0.18 to 0.04 ms and the screen overlay is held on every still frame. A core that cannot draw retained text itself (an older native host) keeps the callbacks that did.
