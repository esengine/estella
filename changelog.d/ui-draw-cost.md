**A blurred text shadow is drawn once per glyph instead of dozens of times, and the screen UI does less work every frame.** A `Text` with `shadowBlur` spread its shadow by stamping the whole label around rings of offsets, up to 64 copies of it, so a screen of labels with a 6 px blur drew every glyph 53 times: on the space frigate's HUD that was 3.2 MB of glyph vertices uploaded and some 28,000 overlapping quads blended every frame, and is now 0.17 MB, with the same number of draw calls. The copies are now composited once, per glyph, size and shadow, into a cell of the glyph atlas and drawn as one quad behind the fill, so the shadow looks as it did; a distance-field glyph's cell is made from its distances at about the size it is shown, and draws in the same batch as the glyph. Bitmap fonts still stamp their copies. The UI layout also stopped solving every change twice (the early pass solved again what the late pass had just solved), and a world anchor reads its camera once a frame instead of once per anchor.
