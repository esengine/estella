**Every kind of water body in the Create menu.** Water Body Ocean, Lake, River, Island and Exclusion Volume, and a Water Zone, can each be placed from the Create menu, a river with the Spline it follows. An island and an exclusion volume show their outline in the viewport and take points dragged and added on it as a lake does, and Details offers the same way back to the rectangle of their size.
