**Custom water bodies take the shape of a mesh.** A WaterBody set to **Custom** names a **Shape Mesh**: the triangles of it that face up, seen from above, are the water, and the water stands at the mesh's top. A fountain bowl, a pool of any outline or a trough is its own shape, an L-shaped pool included (not the hull round it), and it is drawn, answered and seen from under like any lake. The mesh is only a shape; nothing draws it. Place one from the Create menu as Water Body Custom.
