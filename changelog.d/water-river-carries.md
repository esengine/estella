---
**What lies on a river is carried by it.** A river's waves and wind ripples already rode its current; its foam texture, the rings
rain makes and the Water shader's detail maps slid in one fixed direction whatever the current, and the ripples a body or a script
sets off spread about the place they started. All of them now ride the current the same way the surface does, and a wake in a river
drifts downstream at the river's speed. Still water and the open sea draw as before, at no extra cost.
