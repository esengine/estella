**Each body of water can look its own from under it.** A WaterBody's new **Underwater** settings choose where the look from under it comes from: the zone's water material, as before, or its own colour, absorption, density and sunlight through the waves. A murky pond and a clear sea in one zone now each look their own once the camera goes under them.
