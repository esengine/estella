**The World panel shows a streamed world as a map, as Unreal's World Partition editor does.** Every cell is drawn from above, lit when loaded in the editor (or held by a running game), with each top-level entity as a dot and the editor camera marked. Drag a box to select cells, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom; right-click to load, unload or frame the selected cells, or to move the camera there. The list of cells is still one click away.
