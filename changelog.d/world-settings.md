**World Settings, as in Unreal.** Window > World Settings opens a tab beside Details with the open scene's world-wide settings. Enable Streaming turns the scene into a streamed world (it makes the scene's World Settings entity and puts a StreamedWorld on it, as one undo step), and its cell size, plane and far-proxy settings follow with the same rows and resets as Details.
