**A world bigger than one server can be split into zones, each its own process, with `esengine/zones`.** One server holds a few hundred players; a larger world is several zones — maps, lines of a map, instances — and players move between them. `ZoneDirectory` lists the zones that are up (each announces itself on a heartbeat; one that stops is gone) and `pick(kind)` finds the line with most room. `PlayerLeases` keeps each player in one zone at a time: a login while another zone holds them is refused for now, and a zone that crashes frees its players when their leases run out. `ZoneHost` runs a zone over a replication server and player records, and `zone.transfer(connectionId, 'town', arrival)` moves a player in an order that guarantees the destination loads the record just written: save, hand the lease over, issue a one-use ticket, then tell the client. A client connected with `dial` follows by itself, dropping the old zone's ghosts and showing its ticket to the new one; `zone.takeArrival(playerId)` gives the destination what the ticket carried, such as a spawn point. Everything lives in one `PersistStore` the zones share. A world seamless across zone borders, and messages between zones, are not part of it.
