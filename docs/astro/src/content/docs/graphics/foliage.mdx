Vegetation is drawn as **cells**: an area is divided into squares, and each square is one mesh containing all its
plants. A field is a few hundred draw calls that culling can drop whole, and each cell is built at the detail its
distance requires.

| Component | Role |
|---|---|
| `Foliage` | Scatters grass or a mesh over an area by rule. |
| `PlacedFoliage` | Stores hand-placed plants as data in one entity, drawn as instances. |
| `FoliageContact` | Makes plants react to an entity: grass parts, placed plants lean, become entities, can be felled or harvested. |
| `WindWake`, `WindBurst` | Push plants with a moving body's wake or an expanding pressure front. |

Foliage is an optional module (`esengine/foliage`). A package includes it when a scene contains `Foliage` or
`PlacedFoliage`, or a script imports the module. It stands on a `Terrain` when the scene has one and needs no terrain
module to stand on a flat floor.

## Component

Add `Foliage` to an entity. The area is centred on it, and its children are the cells. Cells are
[procedural chunks](/docs/world/streaming/#procedural-chunks): grown round the streaming sources (or the eye, in a game
without any, and in the editor's scene view) out to `drawDistance`, nearest first, and released with their meshes beyond
it, so a large area holds only the cells near a viewer. When a field, the ground or the water changes, cells are regrown;
each old cell stays until its replacement is ready. Nothing is baked.

With a [Terrain](/docs/graphics/terrain/overview/) in the scene, plants stand on it, avoid ground that is too steep, and wait for
it to be built. Without one, the area is flat at the entity's height.

### Plant

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `mesh` | The plant to scatter. Empty generates grass blades. |
| `material` | A material on the **Foliage** shader, which provides wind. Empty draws plants static in their own colours. |
| `minScale`, `maxScale` | Range of per-plant scale. |
| `colorMap`, `colorMapStrength`, `colorMapFrom` | A texture over the area that colours plants by position, mixed in by its alpha: one mesh and material can be pink in one place and yellow in another. `colorMapFrom` is the height up the plant where colouring begins (0 the whole plant; 0.8 only the top of a mesh whose vertex red rises to its petals). Requires a material. Read every frame; changing it regrows nothing. |
| `palette` | A strip of colours from which each plant takes the colour of its top, by a per-plant number: each colour's share of the strip is its share of the plants. The top begins at the material's `u_tipFrom`. Requires a material. |

### Placement

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `densityMap` | Texture whose red channel is plant density: none at black, all of `count` at white. Import **Use**: Data. Empty grows plants everywhere. |
| `scaleMap` | Texture whose red channel is plant size: full at white, smaller toward black, none at black. Import **Use**: Data. A density map alone leaves a ragged edge of full-height tufts; a scale map shortens the grass as it runs out, giving a soft edge. |
| `minUp` | Minimum up-facing component of the ground normal. Lower allows plants on slopes. |
| `heightMin`, `heightMax` | Band of world heights where plants grow. |
| `waterDepth` | Maximum water depth a plant may stand in. 0 (default) keeps plants on dry ground; raise it for reeds. |
| `zoneWorld`, `zoneTags`, `zonePadding` | Zone exclusions; see [Budget and exclusions](#budget-and-exclusions). |

### Area

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `sizeX`, `sizeZ` | Area size, centred on the entity. |
| `cellSize` | Side of a cell. Each cell is one mesh, culled whole. |
| `count` | Plants **per cell**. |
| `seed` | Another seed grows the same plants in other places. |

### Grass

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `bladeHeight`, `bladeWidth` | Blade height and base width at scale 1. |
| `bladeLean` | How far a blade's tip leans over, as a share of its height, each blade in its own direction (default 0.12). Upright blades leave the ground visible between them; leaning blades cover it. |

### Distance and far versions

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `lodDistance` | Distance at which a cell switches to its first far version. |
| `drawDistance` | Distance beyond which a cell is not drawn. Plants thin out one by one before it, so the field has no edge. |
| `castShadows` | Whether cells cast shadows (default on). Turn off for dense short grass to avoid drawing every cell into each shadow cascade; lighting and received shadows remain. |
| `farLevels` | Number of far versions (1–3). |
| `farKeep` | Share of plants each far version keeps of the previous one (default 0.34). |
| `farStep` | How many times further out each far version takes over than the previous (default 2). |
| `farGrow` | Maximum scale-up of a kept plant, to cover the ground of those dropped (default 2). |
| `farGrowUp` | 0–1. Share of that growth applied to a mesh plant's height (default 0). At 0 a kept plant grows across the ground only (a clump of flowers is no taller than one flower); at 1 it grows uniformly. Grass grows broader, never taller. |
| `farTilt` | Degrees by which the top tenth of a mesh plant (a flower head on its stem) is tipped in far versions, each plant in its own direction (default 0). From a low eye a field of upward-facing flowers is seen edge-on; tipped, half the field shows its face. The stem stays upright. |
| `faceEyeNear`, `faceEye` | 0–1. How far the head (top tenth) of a mesh plant turns toward the eye: `faceEyeNear` close up, `faceEye` from half the draw distance out, interpolated between. Above 0, a second UV carries each head vertex's offset from the plant axis and the Foliage shader rotates the head about it. Requires a material. |
| `farCards` | A mesh's far version is crossed cards of its texture (on: trees) or the plant itself, fewer and larger (off: flowers, ferns). |
| `cardU0`, `cardV0`, `cardU1`, `cardV1` | Texture rectangle shown on the crossed cards (left, bottom, right, top). |
| `cardTent` | Card outline: 0 rectangle, 1 tent (conifer). |

Every plant has a number of its own; plants thin by it with distance, and a far version holds exactly the plants still
drawn where it takes over, so nothing jumps at the switch. With a material, each far version is a layer of cells of its
own, grown only as far out as it is drawn: the full meadow exists near the eye and a fraction of it far away. Without a
material, each cell holds its versions and a `LODGroup` selects one, with the distances converted to the screen sizes
it compares. The quality level's **Detail distance** scales `lodDistance` and `drawDistance` (1 at High, 0.8 at Medium,
0.5 at Low).

Near, grass is a bent, tapered blade per plant; far versions use fewer, broader, flat blades. A mesh is copied in full
near; with `farCards`, its far version is two crossed cards per plant.

A distant blade can be narrower than a pixel, so the samples of the picture land on part of it and a far meadow breaks
into dashes. The Foliage shader draws each blade at least `u_strandMinPx` pixels wide (default 1.2) and reduces its
coverage to the share of that width it really has, so with multisampling a far blade fades instead. 0 leaves blades at
their true width.

## Budget and exclusions

`count` is **per cell**. Before distance and terrain rejection, a 700 × 700 field with 35-unit cells and 750 plants per
cell has 400 cells and up to 300 000 plants. Review density (`count / cellSize²`), cell count, near and far distances and
shadow passes together. Larger cells reduce submissions but cull more coarsely. Short grass can set
`castShadows: false`.

When a `WorldGen` exists, Foliage respects its exclusion zones:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `zoneWorld` | WorldGen world whose building and road zones the field respects. Empty uses the first world. |
| `zoneTags` | Extra comma-separated tags for zone exclusions. `foliage` and `grass` (or `tree` for a mesh) are included automatically. |
| `zonePadding` | Extra clearance from zones, in world units. |

Reserve building floors, steps and entrances with `WorldZone` boxes, and roads with zones from `roadZones` / `setRoad`
in `esengine/worldgen`. Moving or deleting a zone regrows the field. Without a WorldGen the field ignores zones.

The editor MCP tool `audit_vegetation` reports authored upper bounds and live counters. Warm generation with `step`
before measuring. `gpuDraws` includes shadow and other passes, while `drawCalls` counts scene lists. Background-window
idle time is not a foreground frame-rate measurement. For large forests, `WorldRules.plants` and `PlacedFoliage` share
geometry and support per-instance LOD.

## Wind

The **Foliage** shader (`builtin:foliage`) bends each vertex by weights painted in its vertex colour: red is the height
up the plant (the root stays, the tip moves), green is a per-plant phase (so a meadow does not sway in unison). A gust
travels across the world and a flutter is added on top.

| Parameter | Description |
|---|---|
| `u_windStrength`, `u_windSpeed` | Bend amount and wind speed. A `Weather` with the Wind drive scales `u_windStrength`. |
| `u_windDirection` | Wind heading in degrees. |
| `u_gustScale` | Gust scale; lower is longer gusts. |
| `u_flutter` | High-frequency flutter. |
| `u_translucency`, `u_translucencyColor` | Light transmitted through the two-sided plant, and its colour (a leaf glows yellower from behind than from the front). |
| `u_specular` | Reflection of sun and sky. |
| `u_bladeFaceEye` | How far a grass blade turns its flat side toward the eye, so it is not a line when seen edge-on. |
| `u_albedo`, `u_useTexture`, `u_alphaCutoff` | Leaf texture with alpha, cut out of a card. |
| `u_rootOcclusion` | Share of environment light reaching a plant's root, rising to all at the tip (default 1: no dimming). Dims ambient and sky light only; the sun and other lights are unaffected. |
| `u_tipFrom` | Height up the plant where the `palette` colour begins. |
| `u_strandMinPx` | Minimum blade width in pixels; see above. |

A custom shader can read the same weights: a vertex hook (`#pragma vertexoffset`) sees the vertex colour as
`es_vertexColor` and the mesh's second UV as `es_vertexUv1` (zero on a mesh without one), which carries each head
vertex's offset from the plant axis when `faceEye` requests it.

## Placed plants

`Foliage` scatters plants by rule. An authored scene has each plant where the artist put it, often tens of thousands of
them; as entities, each would cost a transform, a renderer and a draw. **`PlacedFoliage`** stores them as data in one
entity: a table of `kinds` and a list of `records`.

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `sizeX`, `sizeZ`, `cellSize` | Area centred on the entity, and cell side. |
| `kinds` | Plant kinds; see below. |
| `records` | Six numbers per plant: kind index, x, y, z in the entity's space, rotation about the vertical in radians, and scale. |
| `stateKey` | Name under which a save stores what became of these plants. Empty uses the entity's name. |

| Kind property | Description |
|---|---|
| `parts` | Parts of the plant: `mesh`, a cheaper `lod1Mesh`, an optional cheaper still `lod2Mesh`, `material`, `texture`, `tint`, `opaque` (false for blended parts, drawn after solid ones), optional `soft` (0–1, turns normals up so a card standing for a crown is lit as a rounded shape) and `castShadows` (default true). |
| `lodDistance` | Distance past which `lod1Mesh` is drawn. |
| `lod2Distance` | Distance past which `lod2Mesh` is drawn (default 0: none). |
| `drawDistance` | Distance past which the kind is not drawn as itself. |
| `far`, `farDistance` | Far parts (hierarchical LOD) drawn from `drawDistance` out to `farDistance`. |
| `farKeep` | Share of plants the far version draws, above 0 to 1 (default 1). |
| `interaction` | What the kind does when something comes near; see [Touching plants](#touching-plants). |

Cells round the streaming sources (or the eye) are grown and released with distance. A cell appears at once and fills in
over the following frames, each kind and part taking a few microseconds of the frame budget, nearest first. Each part is
one entity that draws the part's mesh, shared by every cell, at every plant position: a **`MeshInstances`** component
holds the positions (64 bytes per plant), and the engine culls each plant against the view, selects the cheaper meshes
past their distances and draws nothing past `drawDistance`. The geometry exists once however many plants there are, and
draws sharing a mesh and material are one draw. Meshes keep every authored channel (normal map, second UV, vertex
colour); nothing is overwritten, so plants are drawn with ordinary materials, not the Foliage wind shader.

The far version is hierarchical LOD: a tree's crossed cards, baked to their own atlas, as a set of placements drawn from
`drawDistance` out to `farDistance`. It is cheap enough for the whole area to be drawn at once. A kind without a far
version is not drawn past `drawDistance`.

A kind with no cards of its own can use its cheaper mesh as its far version and set `farKeep`: every n-th plant is kept,
n being the nearest whole number to 1 / `farKeep`, each scaled by √n (at most twofold) so the covered ground is
unchanged. Distant ground cover then costs a fraction of its near cost, and the ground texture carries the rest.

Plants stand upright and scale uniformly; records carry no tilt. Paths in `kinds` are project paths, listed so a package
includes them.

## Touching plants

Only plants near something can be touched, so plants need not be entities. Add **`FoliageContact`** to a character (or
anything plants should react to).

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `radius` | Distance from the entity within which plants lean away, in world units. |
| `strength` | Lean amount (0–2): 1 is a full lean, 0 none. |
| `recovery` | Seconds grass takes to stand up again behind the contact. |
| `enabled` | Off: neither bends plants nor activates them. |

**Grass.** A `Foliage` cell is one mesh, so bending is computed per vertex: the Foliage shader receives four positions
(`u_contact0`–`u_contact3`) and moves each blade away from them in proportion to its height up the blade, less the
further it stands above or below. The four are the contacts nearest the streaming source, then the places they recently
left, which close over `recovery`, so a character leaves a parting that closes behind it. `u_contactBend` is how far a
blade parts, as a share of the contact's reach. Contacts beyond the four part nothing. No setup is required beyond the
component.

**Placed plants.** A kind in `PlacedFoliage` or `WorldRules.plants` opts in with `interaction`. The plant remains a
placement in its `MeshInstances` set; within `activationDistance` of a contact it is also given an entity, a
**`PlantProxy`**, removed when the contact leaves. Rendering never changes hands, so nothing pops. The proxy is what a ray
or collision finds, and game components can be added to it.

| `interaction` field | Description |
|---|---|
| `activationDistance` | Distance from a contact at which a plant becomes an entity, in world units. 0 keeps it data (untouchable). |
| `radius`, `height` | Physics capsule standing on the plant's foot, in mesh units × plant scale. Either 0: no capsule. |
| `bend` | Lean away from a contact, in radians: a shear of the placement with a spring, so any material bends with it and it rustles when released. |
| `health` | Damage required to fell it. 0: cannot be felled by damage. |
| `fallSeconds`, `fallAngle`, `lieSeconds` | Fall duration (default 1.8), fall angle in degrees (default 85), and time lying before it sinks away (default 30). |
| `felledKind`, `harvestedKind` | Kind left when felled (a stump) or when nothing is left to harvest (a bare bush): an index into `kinds` or a plant name in `WorldRules.plants`. Absent leaves nothing. |
| `yields` | Number of times `harvest` can take from it. |
| `regrowSeconds` | Seconds after felling or the last harvest before it grows back from small. 0: never. |

Plants are reached through `PlantInteractionRes`:

```ts
import { PlantInteractionRes } from 'esengine/foliage';

const plants = app.getResource(PlantInteractionRes);

// Plants within reach of a swing, nearest first.
const [tree] = plants.near(player.x, player.z, 2);
if (tree) plants.damage(tree, 25, { x: player.x, z: player.z });   // falls away from the blow at zero health

// The plant a ray hit.
const hit = physics.raycast(eye, forward);
const plant = hit && plants.of(hit.entity);
if (plant) inventory.berries += plants.harvest(plant, 1);

plants.on((event) => { if (event.type === 'landed') spawnDust(event.plant); });
```

| Method | Description |
|---|---|
| `near(x, z, radius)` | Plants within the radius, nearest first. |
| `of(entity)` | The plant a `PlantProxy` entity stands for, or `null`. |
| `damage(plant, amount, from?)` | Applies damage; at zero health the plant falls away from `from`. |
| `harvest(plant, amount?)` | Takes up to `amount` yields and returns how many were taken. |
| `on(listener)` | Subscribes to events: `activated`, `deactivated`, `damaged`, `felled`, `landed`, `harvested`, `removed`, `regrown`. Returns an unsubscribe function. |
| `saveState()`, `loadState(state)` | Serialises and restores plant state. |

A felled plant comes down as an entity with its own meshes and a kinematic body rotating about its foot, so it blocks
what it lies across. Its placement is removed at once, including from the far version.

**Persistence.** A plant's state is keyed by the plant, not its cell, so a felled tree stays felled when its chunk is
released and regrown. `saveState()` returns all of it as plain data, including regrowth timers; `loadState(state)`
restores it, before or after cells are grown. A generated world's plants are identified by their place in its plan; a
`PlacedFoliage`'s by their index in `records` under its `stateKey` (or entity name), so a save survives everything except
reordering records. Give two fields different names.

## Wakes and blasts

A contact parts plants while beside them. A fast body also pushes the air: a plant it passes is pushed over, springs back
past upright, and settles after the body has gone; a blast reaches a plant only when its front has travelled that far.
Both are an impulse at a known time, so each plant's response is a damped spring evaluated where the plant is drawn from
the time since the impulse alone: over in about a quarter of a second, back past upright in a third of that, still within
two seconds.

| Component | Field | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **`WindWake`** | `radius` | Distance to each side of the path within which the body pushes the air, in world units (default 2). |
| | `strength` | Push as a share of the body's speed: 1 moves the air as fast as the body (default 1). |
| **`WindBurst`** | `strength` | Front push, in world units per second (default 20). |
| | `speed` | Outward speed of the front (default 40). |
| | `reach` | Distance the front travels before it is spent (default 30). |

A `WindWake` emits a pass every half radius it travels; a `WindBurst` fires once, when its entity gains the component.
From code, `emitWindBurst(app, position, { strength, speed, reach })` starts a front without an entity. The newest eight
passes and four fronts are sent to every material that reads them: the Foliage shader leans plants by `u_wakeBend` (world
units of lean per world unit per second of air push) up to `u_wakeMax`, and the 2D **Sway** template (`sprite-sway`)
leans a sprite's tip along x by its own `u_wakeBend`. `windLean` performs the same calculation on the CPU for anything a
game leans itself.

## Limits

- **Position only.** The area is assumed unrotated and unscaled; the terrain's position and uniform scale are followed,
  rotation is not.
- **Only placed plants can be felled.** Scattered `Foliage` parts for a contact but is one mesh per cell, so it has no
  individual plants to collide with, fell or harvest. A forest that must be cut is a `PlacedFoliage` or
  `WorldRules.plants`.
- **Falling is scripted, not simulated.** A plant rotates about its foot to `fallAngle` and does not settle on slopes or
  roll, so on steep ground a trunk can float above or sink into the ground. Its body is kinematic: it pushes what it lands
  on and nothing pushes it.
- **Collision requires the 3D physics module.** A proxy's capsule is added when the module is loaded (a scene with a
  `CharacterController3D` or a 3D body loads it). Without it, plants still lean and can be felled, but nothing collides. A
  capsule is the only shape: a wide crown or a fork is not followed.
- **A falling plant is saved as felled.** A tree still coming down is saved as felled and is gone on load.
- **A mesh's far version is a card, not an impostor.** It shows the named texture rectangle, identical from every side;
  a plant whose texture has no such picture shows its texture there.
- **Cells are grown within the frame budget,** nearest first, so the distance fills in over a moment.
