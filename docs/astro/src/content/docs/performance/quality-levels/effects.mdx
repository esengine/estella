The groups Global Illumination & Occlusion, Reflections, Sky & Volumetrics and Post-Processing decide which post-process
effects run at each level and how much work each one does. Defaults are given as Low / Medium / High; the key is the field
name in `features.rendering.quality.profiles.<level>`.

## Effects each level runs

Every post-process effect is ticked per level in the group it belongs to. An unticked effect is skipped at that level
wherever a camera's volume requests it, completely: an effect with several passes, such as ambient occlusion or auto
exposure, is removed whole. The profile stores the effects left out:

```json
"quality": { "mode": "auto", "profiles": { "low": { "skipEffects": ["ssr", "taa", "depthOfField"] } } }
```

| Level | Effects left out by default |
|---|---|
| Low | screen-space reflections, temporal anti-aliasing, depth of field, light shafts, motion blur, lens flare, ambient occlusion (SSAO and GTAO), subsurface scattering, voxel GI, dynamic GI |
| Medium | screen-space reflections, depth of field |
| High | none |

These defaults follow the per-pixel work of each effect; they are not measurements on target devices. The editor's
`fx-cost` check measures each effect's cost on the machine it runs on.

**Post-processing** (`postProcess`, on at every level) is a master switch: off runs no effect at that level, whatever a
camera's volume requests.

### Asking whether an effect runs

`qualityRunsEffect(profile, effect)` reports whether the level in force runs an effect. The skip list is not the whole
rule: a level with post-processing off runs nothing, and a level that uses Temporal anti-aliasing runs TAA whatever it
skips and never runs FXAA. The resolved profile has no `ssao` field; use `qualityRunsEffect(quality.profile, 'ssao')`.

A profile written in the earlier format may contain `"ssao": false`, which is read as `ssao` and `gtao` in the skip list,
or `"ssao": true`, which removes `ssao` from it; the editor writes the list instead the next time that level's effects are
edited. `quality.setPreferences({ ssao: false })` is read the same way.

## Effect resolution

Some effects work at half the picture's size per side and are composited over it. Each has a resolution setting that
multiplies that size: `1` draws it at half the picture per side, `0.5` at a quarter, `0.25` at an eighth, down to the
effect's smallest size, below which its image breaks up. Tone mapping, FXAA, grading and every other full-picture pass
always run at full size.

| Setting | Key | Effect | Group | Smallest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ambient occlusion resolution | `aoResolution` | SSAO and GTAO | Global Illumination & Occlusion | a quarter |
| Screen-space GI resolution | `ssgiResolution` | Screen-space GI | Global Illumination & Occlusion | an eighth |
| Voxel GI gather resolution | `voxelGIResolution` | Voxel GI gather on WebGL2 | Global Illumination & Occlusion | a quarter |
| Fog volume resolution | `fogResolution` | Light in height fog | Sky & Volumetrics | an eighth |
| Lens flare resolution | `lensFlareResolution` | Lens flare | Post-Processing | an eighth |

Each defaults to 0.25 / 0.5 / 1. At Low, ambient occlusion and the voxel gather stop at a quarter and the others draw at an
eighth; at Medium all draw at a quarter; at High as authored. Each can be overridden by a player preference, for example
`quality.setPreferences({ fogResolution: 0.5 })`.

The ambient occlusion term is composited by depth, and the depth tolerance grows as the term is drawn smaller, so a
quarter-size term does not band on ground seen at a grazing angle. Sky clouds and bloom always draw at half size and have
no resolution setting.

## Samples, steps and bakes

How many samples, rays and steps lighting effects take, how large a grid or bake they keep and how often they rebake are
settings of their group. Each shader is compiled once with the High count as its bound and stops early at the level's
count, so changing level compiles nothing. A shortened loop keeps its reach: SSR with 16 steps marches as far as with 48
in longer strides, a GTAO slice or GI ray spreads fewer samples over the same radius, and a smaller voxel grid or distance
field covers the same world with larger cells.

| Setting | Key | Effect | Low / Medium / High |
|---|---|---|---|
| SSAO samples | `ssaoSamples` | SSAO | 6 / 8 / 12 |
| GTAO slices × steps | `gtaoSlices`, `gtaoSteps` | GTAO | 2 × 4 / 2 × 5 / 3 × 6 |
| Ambient occlusion blur radius | `aoBlurRadius` | Blur after SSAO and GTAO, texels per side | 2 / 3 / 3 |
| Screen-space GI rays × steps | `ssgiRays`, `ssgiSteps` | Screen-space GI | 4 × 4 / 6 × 5 / 8 × 6 |
| Screen-space GI denoise width | `ssgiDenoiseKernel` | Bilateral filter that composites screen-space GI | 3 × 3 / 3 × 3 / 5 × 5 |
| Voxel GI grid | `voxelGIGridSize` | Voxel GI on WebGPU, voxels across (half as many high) | 64 / 128 / 128 |
| Voxel GI sun samples | `voxelGISunSamples` | Voxel GI on WebGPU, samples per side of each sun shadow tile | 64 / 128 / 256 |
| Voxel GI bounce rays | `voxelGIBounceRays` | Voxel GI on WebGL2 | 1 / 2 / 2 |
| Cheaper dynamic GI | `dynamicGIReduced` | Dynamic GI | on / on / off |
| Dynamic GI probe rows | `dynamicGIProbeRows` | Dynamic GI | 30 / 45 / 90 |
| Dynamic GI field size | `dynamicGIFieldSize` | Dynamic GI distance field, cells per side of each level | 64 / 128 / 128 |
| Dynamic GI field build budget | `dynamicGIFieldBuildMs` | ms per frame the field rebuilds in after a move (four times this for a scene's first field) | 1 / 2 / 3 |
| Per-pixel light probes | `probesPerPixel` | Probe volume lookup for meshes without lightmap UVs | off / on / on |
| Contact shadow steps | `contactShadowSteps` | Contact Shadows, steps marched per pixel | 8 / 12 / 20 |
| SSR steps + refinement | `ssrSteps`, `ssrRefine` | Screen-space reflections | 16 + 4 / 32 + 5 / 48 + 6 |
| Sky lighting update interval | `skyEnvironmentInterval` | Seconds between bakes of a changing sky | 4 / 2 / 1 |
| Sky lighting cloud steps | `skyEnvironmentCloudSteps` | The sky bake's march through clouds | 8 / 12 / 16 |
| Environment reflection size | `environmentMapSize` | Sky and captured probe reflections, texels per side | 16 / 32 / 32 |
| Probe capture size | `probeCaptureSize` | Each face a capturing light probe volume draws | 16 / 32 / 32 |
| Still sky from a panorama | `stillSky` | A still sky is drawn once into a panorama and read from it, instead of computed per pixel every frame | on / on / off |

- **Cheaper dynamic GI**: one pixel in four queries the probes, a probe traces a quarter of its rays per frame, cards are
  relit over eight frames, and two thirds of the probe rows are used. Light can spread a pixel or two across a shared edge,
  and changing light takes about twice as long to settle.
- **Dynamic GI probe rows** no longer depend on the platform; the cheaper mode keeps two thirds of the level's rows.
- **Per-pixel light probes** off: a mesh reads one probe value at its position (as meshes with lightmap UVs always do)
  and no brick atlas is kept on the GPU, so a long mesh spanning lit and dark areas is lit uniformly.
- GTAO uses at least two slices; with a single slice, the per-pixel slice rotation repeats every other pixel of a pass
  drawn below half size and samples only a few directions, producing bands.
- Low leaves SSR, SSAO, GTAO, voxel GI and dynamic GI out by default, so their Low values matter only to a project that
  enables them at Low. Without temporal anti-aliasing, 16 SSR steps leave a visible stipple in reflections.

### Planar reflections

| Setting | Key | Low / Medium / High | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planar reflection scale | `planarReflectionScale` | 0.25 / 0.5 / 1 | Share of the screen resolution (per side) a planar reflection is drawn at. |
| Planar reflection max size | `planarReflectionMaxSize` | 512 / 1024 / 2048 | Most texels per side, whatever the scale gives on a large screen. |
| Planar reflection updates / s | `planarReflectionUpdatesPerSecond` | 30 / 0 / 0 | Redraws per second; `0` is every frame. Never caps the main scene. |

While a level is in force, a planar reflection set to its own values (**Custom**) is held under the level's: its scale and
size no larger than the level's, and, where the level sets an update rate, drawn no more often (a reflection drawn only on
request is unaffected). The author's values can only make it cheaper. With no level in force a Custom reflection keeps
its own values ([Planar Reflections](/docs/graphics/lighting/planar-reflections/)).

## Steps and samples

Effects that march a ray or gather a ring of taps take their count from the level. Each shader is compiled for High's
count and stops at the level's; fewer steps are longer steps over the same distance, so a cloud is as deep, a light shaft
as long and a blur as wide. What is lost is smoothness: grain where a jittered march has too few steps, or a horizon
slightly off where the sky's air is integrated in fewer pieces.

| Setting | Key | Counts | Group | Low / Medium / High |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fog light steps | `fogVolumeSteps` | Steps of sunlight through height fog | Sky & Volumetrics | 8 / 16 / 24 |
| Fog cloud samples | `fogCloudSamples` | Points up through the clouds per fog step | Sky & Volumetrics | 2 / 3 / 4 |
| Cloud steps | `cloudSteps` | Steps of a sky ray through the main cloud layer | Sky & Volumetrics | 12 / 14 / 16 |
| Upper cloud steps | `cloudUpperSteps` | Steps through the upper cloud layer | Sky & Volumetrics | 3 / 4 / 6 |
| Cloud shadow samples | `cloudShadowSamples` | Points along the sun's ray for cloud shadows on the ground | Sky & Volumetrics | 3 / 4 / 6 |
| Sky scattering steps | `skyScatterSteps` | Steps the sky colour is integrated in | Sky & Volumetrics | 8 / 12 / 16 |
| Sky sunlight steps | `skyLightDepthSteps` | Steps from a sky point to the sun | Sky & Volumetrics | 3 / 3 / 4 |
| Depth of field samples | `dofSamples` | Taps a blurred pixel gathers | Post-Processing | 16 / 32 / 64 |
| Light shaft samples | `lightShaftSamples` | Points a pixel samples towards the sun | Post-Processing | 16 / 24 / 40 |
| Motion blur samples | `motionBlurSamples` | Points a moving pixel is smeared over | Post-Processing | 6 / 10 / 16 |
| Subsurface scattering samples | `subsurfaceSamples` | Taps per side in each of the two passes | Post-Processing | 3 / 4 / 6 |
| Refraction blur samples | `refractionSamples` | Taps of the scene behind a rough transmissive surface | Effects & Particles | 4 / 6 / 8 |

- Low leaves depth of field, light shafts, motion blur and subsurface scattering out by default, so their Low counts
  matter only to a project that enables them at Low.
- The cloud march starts each pixel at a 4 × 4 ordered offset, which the composite's four-texel read evens out.

**Bloom levels** (`bloomLevels`, Post-Processing; 3 / 4 / 5) is how many times bloom halves the bright image before
building it back up; at 5 it reaches a sixty-fourth of the picture. One level fewer drops the smallest down and up steps,
so the glow reaches less far around a light; the remaining steps cost the same.

### Counts in materials

A material reads its count from the engine rather than from its own parameters: the frame's `u_quality` constants,
whose first slot is the refraction count. A setting claims a slot with `materialSlot`. A slot holding zero, which is its
value with the quality system off, means the maximum count.
