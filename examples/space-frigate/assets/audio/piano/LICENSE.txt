The recordings in this folder are notes of "Upright Piano KW" (small, version 2019-07-03),
a Kawai upright piano recorded by Gonzalo and Roberto for the FreePats project:
http://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html#UprightKW

They were taken out of its SoundFont one note to a file and halved to 22050 samples a second.
